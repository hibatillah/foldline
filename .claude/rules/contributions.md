# Contributions

The project is open source under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (`LICENSE`). Outside contributions are welcome through forks and pull
requests; keep `ONBOARDING.md` to setup, structure and the rules the code follows. There are no accounts, paid tiers or
permissions: every page and activity is available to everyone, and neither program contacts a Foldline server.

- `CONTRIBUTING.md` holds the contributor agreement (GPL license, the maintainer's right to distribute free of charge
  through the Microsoft Store, no profit from contributions); `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` repeats it with a box to
  tick. Never remove either, and never accept code that is not under the agreement.
- Foldline is free of charge everywhere: no price, paid tier, in-app purchase or advertising.
- The name and logo are not part of the code license (`TRADEMARK.md`). A change adds no other product name or logo to the app.
